I'd like to develop an application for governance, risk and compliance that is a SaaS-based program that has the following features.

1. Enterprise Risk Management (ERM)
• Centralized Risk Registers: Consolidates operational, financial, cyber, and strategic risks across all global business units into one database.
• Risk Scoring & Quantification: Uses configurable heat maps, Key Risk Indicators (KRIs), and financial modeling (like FAIR) to measure potential business impact.
• Scenario & Sensitivity Analysis: Runs simulations to see how major disruptions, such as market shifts or cyberattacks, would affect the organization.

2. Compliance & Regulatory Change Management

• Regulatory Horizon Scanning: Integrates with regulatory feeds to automatically alert teams when laws change in specific countries or industries.
• Multi-Framework Mapping: Maps a single internal control to multiple regulations (e.g., GDPR, SOX, ISO 27001, HIPAA) so teams only test and document it once.
• Continuous Control Monitoring (CCM): Connects directly to IT infrastructure to verify in real time that security controls are working properly.

3. Policy & Governance Management

• End-to-End Policy Lifecycle: Automates the drafting, legal review, version control, approval, and scheduled retirement of corporate policies.
• Employee Attestation Tracking: Distributes policies to employees and tracks who has read, acknowledged, and passed related compliance training.
• Exception Management: Provides a formal workflow to request, evaluate, approve, and track temporary exceptions to company rules.

4. Internal Audit Management

• Risk-Based Audit Planning: Uses live risk data to help audit teams prioritize which departments or systems need review most urgently.
• Automated Workpapers & Evidence: Collects digital evidence automatically and organizes audit schedules, findings, and sign-offs in one workspace.
• Issue & Remediation Tracking: Assigns corrective actions to business owners with automated deadline reminders and verification steps.

5. Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM)

• Vendor Onboarding & Tiering: Evaluates and categorizes suppliers based on how much data access or operational criticality they hold.
• Automated Security Questionnaires: Sends, collects, and scores vendor risk assessments and tracks third-party certifications like SOC 2 reports.
• Continuous Vendor Monitoring: Integrates with external threat intelligence to alert teams if a supplier suffers a data breach or financial downturn.

6. Business Continuity & Operational Resilience

• Business Impact Analysis (BIA): Identifies critical business processes and calculates the maximum tolerable downtime for each.
• Disaster Recovery & Crisis Plans: Stores actionable playbooks and emergency communication workflows to keep operations running during an outage.

7. Enterprise Architecture & Reporting

• Executive Dashboards: Delivers real-time, role-based analytics and board-ready reports that link risk directly to business performance.
• Deep Tech Stack Integrations: Connects via APIs to ERP systems (like SAP), ITSM platforms (like ServiceNow), HR software, and cloud providers (AWS, Azure).
• Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Enforces strict permissions so sensitive audit findings or legal risks are only visible to authorized personnel.
